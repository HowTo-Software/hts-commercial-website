# Code of Conduct

> Status: Draft team guidance; HTS review pending. Owner: HTS team. Historical project baseline: 2026-09-22. Current edition: Unreleased.

Project collaboration should remain respectful, constructive and accessible to people of different backgrounds, experience levels and abilities. Discuss implementation choices with evidence, explain disagreement clearly and accept corrective feedback.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized remarks, intimidation and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not expose customer briefs, contact details or credentials while discussing an issue.

This guidance applies to repository discussions, reviews and communication when representing HTS. Report concerns privately to a known HTS maintainer through the team's established channel. A public contact address or enforcement schedule has not been configured for this repository; the HTS team must define those before opening a public contribution community.

The HTS team reviews reports, seeks relevant context and may request changes, restrict participation or remove harmful content. Protect reporter privacy and avoid public accusations while a report is being assessed.

**PT-BR:** colaboração respeitosa, feedback construtivo e proteção de dados pessoais são obrigatórios. Relate problemas em particular a um responsável conhecido da HTS. Este texto é orientação em revisão, sem contato ou prazo de resposta inventado.
