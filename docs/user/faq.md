# Customer FAQ

> Status: Draft, pending HTS review. Owner: HTS team. Historical frontend baseline: 2026-10-02. Current edition: Unreleased.

## Does this tool build my website automatically?

No. It prepares a structured custom-website brief for a later discussion with HTS. The site describes services and demonstrative concepts, not completed customer work or automatic generation.

## Did HTS receive my request after I downloaded the brief?

No. Downloading saves a TXT on your device. There is no delivery API, email sender or database submission in this version. Share it through a verified team contact channel; no contact address is invented by this guide.

## Can HTS maintain a website built by another team?

The maintenance option supports an existing project and an optional current website address. Include goals and relevant context; the actual service scope must be assessed by HTS.

## Do I need to know every technical detail?

No. State your goals and use uncertainty choices where available. Budget, timeline and hosting answers express your preferences; they are not binding prices or commitments.

## Can I upload a logo or reference images?

The wizard asks what materials you have. There is no file-upload facility in this iteration. Provide textual descriptions and safe reference website addresses, without credentials.

## Are my answers saved for another visit?

The draft is held in the current server-side Blazor circuit. A reload, expired session or restart can discard it. Theme/language preferences are separate and do not save your brief. Download a copy before leaving.

## Can I pay or create an account here?

This project has no account or payment workflow. Discuss a proposal directly with HTS. Never enter passwords, card details or hosting secrets into the brief.
