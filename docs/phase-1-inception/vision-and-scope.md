# Vision and scope

> Status: Draft, pending HTS review. Owner: HTS team. Historical project baseline: 2026-09-22. Current edition: Unreleased.

## Problem and intended outcome

Customers often describe a website only as “a business site” or “a store,” omitting goals, audience, pages, visual preferences and integrations. HTS needs a structured brief before discussing scope, feasibility, delivery and pricing. Hank requested a wizard or question list for customers to explain how they want their custom website.

The site presents HTS services and helps prospects prepare this information without replacing the current frontend. Primary users are owners of small businesses, professionals and teams requesting a new website or improvements to an existing project. The HTS team reviews the eventual brief; it owns delivery decisions and commercial commitments.

## Scope

Include service-prefill behavior, five guided steps, clear required/optional fields, safe existing-site references, responsive controls, localized validation and a reviewable UTF-8 export. Keep project examples visibly demonstrative and retain the existing themes and navigation.

Automatic website generation, quotes with binding prices, payments, accounts, file uploads, CRM delivery and durable draft storage are outside this iteration. A future delivery integration requires its own privacy, authorization, retention and abuse-control design.

## Success criteria

A visitor can explain the requested site, move backward without losing circuit state, correct validation errors and download all answers in the selected interface language. The page must explain that HTS has not received the request. The main risks are lost drafts after connection changes, misleading submission copy and a long form becoming difficult on mobile.
