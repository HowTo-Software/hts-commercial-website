# Privacy, licensing and accessibility responsibilities

> Status: Draft responsibilities, not legal/compliance certification. Owner: HTS team. Historical frontend baseline: 2026-10-02. Current edition: Unreleased.

## Personal information

The brief collects contact and business/project context. Although it is not saved to a database or sent by email, server-interactive Blazor processes it in memory and the visitor may download a copy. The production notice must explain that handling, preference cookie/browser storage, circuit lifetime, relevant host logs and the actual contact channel. Do not request credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.

HTS must assess applicable privacy obligations for its audience and hosting arrangements, including LGPD where applicable. A legal basis, retention policy, data-subject request contact and processor arrangements cannot be invented by a documentation template. Delivery/storage changes require that assessment to be updated before launch.

## Accessibility and evidence

Preserve labels, keyboard navigation, dialog behavior, focus visibility, readable contrast, zoom support and reduced motion. Verify them through actual [cases](../phase-4-testing/test-cases.md); do not claim WCAG certification without an audit. Record real review evidence and approvals separately from historical project baseline dates.
